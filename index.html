One assessment. Every partner company.

Instead of sitting five separate company screening processes, a final-year student writes one standardized, multi-round assessment. The score report goes to partner companies, who use it to shortlist or to waive parts of their own screening.

How scores are produced

The analytical and behavioural rounds are scored against a fixed answer key. The written round is scored against a five-criterion rubric, and a human reviewer confirms or overrides every rubric score before it reaches a company. No score is shared with any company unless the candidate has consented.

This prototype ships with a synthetic reference cohort so that percentiles are meaningful from the first attempt. Those rows are labelled as synthetic throughout.
